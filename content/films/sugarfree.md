After losing someone dear, a young woman gives up dancing and moves from the city to the suburbs. She tries to move through a life shaped by performance, control, and absence.

## From the director

To me, ballet holds a contradiction I deeply relate to: it is elegant and disciplined, but also fragile, punishing, and built around the demand to appear effortless.
It became a way to visualize the tension between control and collapse, beauty and pain, performance and emptiness.
What happens when the ways we learn to survive begin to consume us—and is there still a way to return to ourselves?

Zebra follows Leslie and Isabel, two people who have been together for so long that somewhere along the way, they forgot how to love each other. Leslie retreats inward, while Isabel searches elsewhere for the feeling of being seen.

## From the director

I made Zebra out of a feeling that my generation is learning how to achieve and adapt faster than we are learning how to love.

The zebra became a way for me to hold that feeling: displaced, marked by the world it has moved through, never quite belonging anywhere.

When everything around us keeps changing, can we still learn to see the people closest to us?

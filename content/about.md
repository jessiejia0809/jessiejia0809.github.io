I’m Jessie Jia, a researcher and filmmaker working at the intersection of human–AI interaction, creative practice, and emerging media.

I study how AI changes the way we make, imagine, and tell stories.

I graduated from Cornell University in 2026 with a bachelor’s degree in computer science. My research asks how AI can participate in making, experimentation, judgment, and expression while preserving human agency, craft, and, most importantly, love.

My films ask how we speak about the world by translating larger social and political questions into intimate stories of family, love, and human relationships.

Outside of research, you can most likely find me in theaters or at film festivals. My favorite theater is absolutely Metrograph in the Lower East Side, Manhattan.
